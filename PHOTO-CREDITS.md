# Photo credits

Photography is sourced from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/) and stored locally as WebP files so the published pages do not depend on the Unsplash image CDN at runtime. Photo IDs and the download/resize settings are maintained in [`tools/prepare_stock_photos.ps1`](./tools/prepare_stock_photos.ps1).

The photos are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The store hero, 20 category images, and four product images are local files in `assets/images/`.
